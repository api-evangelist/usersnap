TL;DR Roadmapping tools like Productboard, Aha!, Canny, and Roadmunk were designed to display your priorities. Teams are frustrated because the problem isn’t showing; it’s deciding. Getting clarity on impact, effort, readiness, and balance against company, product, and team goals is hell.
